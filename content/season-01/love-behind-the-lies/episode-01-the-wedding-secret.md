# LOVE BEHIND THE LIES
## Episode 1: THE WEDDING SECRET
**Original screenplay · NEXORA AI DRAMA · Draft 1**

**Target runtime:** 4–6 minutes  
**Format:** 16:9 episode; dialogue-led scenes suitable for AI-assisted visual production  
**Content note:** Romantic mystery; no graphic violence.

### SCENE 1 — INT. VALE HOUSE, BRIDAL SUITE — NIGHT

A small wedding reception has ended. Rain taps against tall windows. A gold wedding band rests on a dressing table beside a half-empty glass of water.

MARA REYES stands alone, still in her wedding dress. She removes a hairpin and notices a narrow envelope tucked inside the bouquet.

She opens it.

Inside: an old photograph. Three people stand outside a building. One is her father. Another is a younger ADRIAN VALE. The date on the back is seven days before the fire that destroyed her family's business.

MARA (quietly)
No. That can't be.

The door opens. ADRIAN enters, loosening his tie.

ADRIAN
I thought you'd be downstairs.

Mara holds up the photograph.

MARA
You knew my father.

Adrian stops. The room goes still.

**CUT TO TITLE: LOVE BEHIND THE LIES**

### SCENE 2 — INT. BRIDAL SUITE — CONTINUOUS

Adrian approaches, but Mara steps back.

ADRIAN
Where did you find that?

MARA
In my bouquet. Someone wanted me to see it. So I'll ask again. How did you know my father?

ADRIAN
I worked for a company that was negotiating with him.

MARA
You told me you met him once.

ADRIAN
I did.

MARA
This picture says otherwise.

Adrian looks at the date. His expression changes.

ADRIAN
Your father asked me for help.

MARA
What kind of help?

ADRIAN
He believed someone was trying to take his business. He had documents. He said if anything happened, I should make sure you were safe.

MARA
And you decided the best way to do that was to marry me without telling me?

ADRIAN
No. I decided to stay close because I—

He stops himself.

MARA
Because you what?

ADRIAN
Because I made him a promise.

### SCENE 3 — INT. VALE HOUSE, HALLWAY — NIGHT

Mara leaves the suite with the photograph. Adrian follows but keeps his distance.

MARA
A promise made to my father doesn't give you the right to choose what I know.

ADRIAN
You're right.

That answer catches her off guard.

ADRIAN (CONT'D)
I should have told you before the wedding. I was afraid the truth would put you in danger.

MARA
You don't get to call a lie protection.

A phone vibrates on a console table. The screen lights up with an unknown message preview:

**UNKNOWN:** *She found the photograph. Do not tell her about the second copy.*

Adrian sees it. He quickly turns the phone face down.

Mara notices.

MARA
Who is that?

ADRIAN
No one you need to worry about.

MARA
That's the second lie tonight.

### SCENE 4 — INT. REYES FAMILY HOME, KITCHEN — LATER THAT NIGHT

Mara calls her aunt ELENA. The call goes to voicemail. Mara leaves a message.

MARA
Aunt Elena, I found a photograph. Dad was with Adrian before the fire. Please call me. I need to know what happened.

She hangs up. A new message arrives from an unknown number. It contains a photograph of the same image—but the crop reveals a third person standing just outside the frame.

Text beneath it:

**UNKNOWN:** *Ask your husband who paid for the silence.*

Mara stares at the screen.

### SCENE 5 — INT. VALE HOUSE, BRIDAL SUITE — NIGHT

Adrian stands by the window, speaking softly on the phone.

ADRIAN
She knows about the photograph.

He listens.

ADRIAN
No. I won't keep lying to her.

A pause.

ADRIAN (CONT'D)
Then tell Victor the agreement is over.

He ends the call. Behind him, Mara stands in the doorway. She heard the name.

MARA
Victor? What does he have to do with my father?

Adrian turns. For the first time, he looks genuinely afraid.

ADRIAN
Mara, listen to me—

MARA
No more promises. No more half-truths. Tell me everything.

Adrian opens his mouth, but his phone lights up again.

**UNKNOWN:** *If you tell her, she'll be next.*

Mara sees the message.

**CUT TO BLACK.**
