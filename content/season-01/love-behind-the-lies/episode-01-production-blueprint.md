# LOVE BEHIND THE LIES — Episode 1 Production Blueprint
## The Wedding Secret

**Status:** Production instructions prepared; no rendered video is included in this file.
**Target:** 4–6 minute 16:9 episode, with vertical 9:16 teaser exports.
**Style:** Original cinematic romantic mystery. Keep character designs consistent across all shots.

## 1. Character continuity prompts
Use the same reference image or character seed for every shot featuring each character. Do not imitate real actors or existing film characters.

- **Mara Reyes:** fictional Filipina woman, 27, warm medium complexion, long dark-brown hair, expressive brown eyes, elegant ivory wedding dress, natural makeup; intelligent, hurt but determined. Keep facial features, hair, dress and jewelry consistent throughout the wedding-night sequence.
- **Adrian Vale:** fictional Filipino man, 32, neatly styled black hair, dark eyes, tailored charcoal suit, white shirt, loosened black tie; reserved expression, controlled posture.
- **Elena Reyes:** fictional Filipino woman, early 50s, shoulder-length dark hair with subtle silver strands, practical blouse, caring but guarded expression.
- **Victor Sarmiento:** fictional Filipino man, late 50s, salt-and-pepper hair, refined dark suit, polished public demeanor.

## 3. Dialogue and voice recording
Use the exact dialogue in episode-01-the-wedding-secret.md. Record each character separately if possible. Keep dialogue natural, with pauses after reveals. If using synthetic voices, use voices you have permission to use and avoid cloning real people without consent. Keep music below dialogue; if no licensed track is available, use silence plus rain/room ambience.

## 4. Editing timeline
- 00:00–00:25: rainy house, bridal suite, envelope and photograph.
- 00:25–01:35: Adrian enters; Mara confronts him.
- 01:35–02:25: promise, hallway confrontation and first anonymous message.
- 02:25–03:20: Mara calls Elena; second photograph and warning arrive.
- 03:20–04:30: Adrian's call, Victor's name, final threat.
- 04:30–04:45: cliffhanger and end card.
Extend pauses and dialogue coverage to reach 4–6 minutes without padding or repeating shots.

## 5. Captions and export
- Add accurate captions for all spoken dialogue and meaningful sounds.
- 1920×1080 or 1280×720, 16:9, MP4 H.264, AAC audio for the full episode.
- 1080×1920, 9:16, MP4 H.264 for teasers; keep text inside safe margins.
- Use a readable serif or sans-serif title font with commercial-use permission.
- Check audio levels, spelling, black frames, visual continuity, and phone-message legibility before export.

## 6. Release gate
Do not mark the episode produced until the final MP4 has been exported and watched from beginning to end. Do not mark it published until the public URL and publication date are recorded. Use only original or properly licensed visuals, voices, music, fonts and sound effects. Platform AI-content disclosure rules may apply.

## 7. Free workflow
A phone-based editor with a free tier can assemble stills, voice recordings, captions and ambience. Check current free-tier limits before starting. Do not purchase subscriptions or assets. If a tool adds a watermark, use another free option or accept the watermark rather than paying.
