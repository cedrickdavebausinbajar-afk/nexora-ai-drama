# NEXORA AI DRAMA — Original Series Bible
## LOVE BEHIND THE LIES
**Season 1 · Five episodes · Original fictional story**

### Series premise
On the night of her wedding, Mara Reyes finds a photograph proving that her new husband, Adrian Vale, knew her late father. Adrian admits he has been protecting a secret about the fire that ruined the Reyes family—but insists he married Mara to keep her safe, not to deceive her. As each episode reveals another piece of the past, Mara must decide whether love can survive a truth built on silence.

### Main characters
- **Mara Reyes (27):** A resourceful restoration artist. Observant, compassionate, and unwilling to be controlled.
- **Adrian Vale (32):** A reserved hotel-and-property executive. He carries guilt over a promise made to Mara's father.
- **Elena Reyes (52):** Mara's aunt and guardian. Protective, practical, and hiding a letter she was asked to keep.
- **Victor Sarmiento (58):** A respected investor whose business empire grew after the fire. Charming in public; calculating in private.
- **Noah Cruz (29):** A local journalist and Mara's childhood friend, determined to uncover the official fire report.

### Tone and visual identity
Modern romantic mystery melodrama. Cinematic but achievable with original AI-generated stills/video, voiceover, dialogue cards, and simple motion. Black-and-gold NEXORA branding belongs on title/end cards only; story scenes should have their own consistent palette.

### Five-episode arc
1. **The Wedding Secret** — On her wedding night, Mara finds an old photograph of Adrian with her father, dated the week before the fire. Adrian says he promised her father he would protect her. Mara asks why he never told her.
2. **A Photograph from the Past** — Noah finds a discrepancy in the fire report. Elena admits the photograph was taken at a meeting she attended, but refuses to name the third person in the room.
3. **The Stranger at the Gate** — A stranger leaves Mara a key and a warning: Adrian is not the only person who knows what happened. Mara follows the key to a closed archive.
4. **Promises Under Oath** — Adrian gives a statement that could destroy Victor's company. Mara learns Adrian's family benefited from the fire and questions whether his protection was another form of control.
5. **The Lie Between Us** — Mara exposes the forged report, but the final evidence reveals Elena made a promise of her own. Mara chooses the truth and sets the terms for any future with Adrian.

### Continuity rules
- Mara's father died before the series begins; do not depict him as alive in present-day scenes.
- The photograph is a recurring physical clue: faded color print, three adults outside a rain-streaked office, date written on the back.
- Adrian must not be portrayed as secretly responsible for the fire; his conflict is guilt and concealment, not villainy.
- Victor's role is suggested gradually; do not reveal the full scheme in Episode 1.
- Every episode ends with a clear unanswered question.
- Keep dialogue natural and concise; do not rely on narration to explain what a scene already shows.

### Production format
Recommended first release: 4–6 minute pilot, 16:9 full episode, plus 9:16 teaser cutdowns. These are creative targets, not claims that any video has been produced. Use original or properly licensed visuals, voices, music, and sound effects.
