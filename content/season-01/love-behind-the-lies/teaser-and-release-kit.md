### Publishing checklist
- Export the full episode in 1080p if the device and editor support it.
- Export each teaser in 9:16 with readable captions and safe margins.
- Upload the full episode to YouTube; upload vertical teaser versions to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Facebook Reels.
- Use the same series title and episode numbering on every platform.
- Add a clear disclosure if a platform requires labeling realistic AI-generated or altered content.
- Confirm all generated visuals, voices, music, fonts, and sound effects are original, licensed, or otherwise permitted.
- Never upload a third-party drama episode or use a creator's video without permission.

### Zero-budget release cadence
- Day 1: Full Episode 1 on YouTube.
- Day 2: Teaser A on Shorts, TikTok, and Reels.
- Day 4: Teaser B on all three platforms.
- Day 6: Teaser C on all three platforms.
- Day 7: Reply to comments and review views, average view duration, retention, shares, saves, and new followers.

### What counts as completed
A script and teaser copy are not a produced video. Mark each asset complete only after the final exported video exists, is reviewed, and is published. Record the public URLs and date in a release log.
